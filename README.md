# Finance Analytics Project | SQL Business KPI Analysis

## Project Overview
The Finance Analytics Project uses SQL to analyze financial transactions and evaluate overall financial performance. The objective is to transform raw financial data into meaningful business insights by tracking income, expenses, savings, budgets, cash flow, and account balances.

The project covers 20 Business Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) to support financial monitoring, spending analysis, trend identification, and data-driven decision-making.

## Business Objectives
- Analyze total income, expenses, and net savings.
- Monitor monthly cash flow and financial trends.
- Identify major spending categories and recurring expenses.
- Compare actual expenses against budgeted amounts.
- Evaluate transaction patterns across weekdays and weekends.
- Track account balances and month-over-month growth.
- Develop a consolidated view of financial health.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Database:** MySQL
- **Query Language:** SQL
- **Techniques:** Aggregations, GROUP BY, CASE WHEN, date functions, joins, subqueries, Common Table Expressions (CTEs), and window functions, where applicable.

## Business KPI Questions

1. What is the total income across all accounts?
2. What is the total amount of expenses across all accounts?
3. What is the net savings (total income minus total expenses)?
4. What percentage of income is being saved (savings rate)?
5. What is the monthly cash flow (income vs expenses per month)?
6. How much income is coming from each income source/category?
7. How much is being spent in each expense category?
8. Which expense category has the highest total spending?
9. How does actual spending compare to the budgeted amount for each category?
10. What is the average amount spent per day?
11. What is the trend of total spending month by month?
12. What is the running account balance after each transaction?
13. Which expenses are recurring every month (subscriptions, bills, etc.)?
14. Is spending higher on weekends or on weekdays?
15. Which are the top 10 largest transactions overall?
16. What is the current balance of each account?
17. How much is income growing or shrinking month over month?
18. How much are expenses growing or shrinking month over month?
19. What percentage does each category contribute to total spending?
20. What does the overall financial health dashboard look like (summary of key numbers)?

## Key Analytical Areas

- **Income & Savings Analysis:** Total income, expenses, net savings, and savings rate.
- **Cash Flow Analysis:** Monthly inflows, outflows, and net cash flow.
- **Expense Analysis:** Category-level spending, highest expense categories, and spending distribution.
- **Budget Analysis:** Comparison of actual spending with planned budgets.
- **Transaction Analysis:** Largest transactions, recurring payments, and daily spending patterns.
- **Trend Analysis:** Month-over-month changes in income and expenses.
- **Account Analysis:** Running balances and current balances by account.
- **Financial Reporting:** Consolidated KPIs for an overall financial health dashboard.

## Business Value

This analysis can help stakeholders understand where money is coming from, where it is being spent, how actual spending compares with budgets, and how financial performance changes over time. These insights can support expense control, budgeting, cash flow monitoring, and financial planning.
